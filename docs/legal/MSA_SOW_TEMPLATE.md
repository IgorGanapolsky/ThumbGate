# Master Services Agreement + Statement of Work template (managed services)

**Status:** Template for counsel/product use. Not a signed contract.  
**Public index:** `/legal` · related commercial terms: `/terms`

Use when offering **managed** work (if offered). Self-serve Pro alone is covered by Terms + subscription checkout, not this SOW.

---

## Master Services Agreement (summary clauses)

1. **Parties** — ThumbGate provider and Customer.  
2. **Services** — Only work described in an executed SOW.  
3. **Fees** — Fixed fee or rates in SOW; taxes extra; payment via Stripe or invoice as stated.  
4. **Customer materials** — Customer provides non-secret examples, access, and an accountable owner; delays pause timelines.  
5. **IP** — Pre-existing ThumbGate IP remains ThumbGate’s. Customer owns customer-specific configs delivered under the SOW. ThumbGate may reuse generalized non-confidential know-how.  
6. **Confidentiality** — Mutual; exclude public info and independent development.  
7. **Data** — Prefer local-first; customer controls what leaves their environment; no use of customer secrets in marketing.  
8. **Warranties** — Services performed in a professional manner; **no guarantee** all unsafe agent actions will be blocked.  
9. **Liability** — Cap at fees paid under the applicable SOW in the prior 12 months; no consequential damages to the extent permitted by law.  
10. **Term / termination** — As in SOW; survival of IP, confidentiality, liability.  
11. **Governing law** — As in public Terms unless SOW states otherwise.

## Statement of Work template (fields)

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| SOW title | e.g. Workflow Gate Implementation |
| Customer | Legal name, contact, email |
| Effective date | |
| Fees | Amount, currency, payment method |
| Scope (in) | One supported workflow; one configured gate; regression evidence; rollout/rollback writeup |
| Scope (out) | Multi-workflow, SSO, compliance certs, 24/7 monitoring, guaranteed savings |
| Customer obligations | Owner, access, golden cases, non-secret samples |
| Acceptance | Written sign-off or N business days without material objection |
| Refund / out-of-scope | If not reducible to supported gate: refund rule matching public Terms (if offered) |
| Timeline | Start after materials; target delivery window |
| Support after delivery | None / limited days / separate retainer |
| Security / access | Least privilege; no production secrets in tickets |

## Customer confidentiality (short form)

Customer confidential information is used only to perform the SOW, not for marketing or third-party training, and is returned or deleted on request subject to legal retention of billing records.
