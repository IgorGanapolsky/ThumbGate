## Evidence

Raw Stripe session creation is not buyer intent; a sent link is not checkout; checkout is not payment; payment is not a customer outcome. Only provider-confirmed payments and active subscriptions count. A Stripe Checkout Session, Payment Link, price, product, or raw PayPal checkout URL is not payment evidence.

The private historical workflow ledger requires accepted-scope evidence before `named_pilot` and cannot advance to `paid_team` until the linked sales-pipeline record reconciles. The accepted scope must name the exact offer, catalog value or documented historical balance, USD currency, cadence, workflow count, signer, timestamp, source/reference, and SHA-256 agreement digest. Provider payment evidence cannot be reused across multiple workflow contracts. Enterprise recurring service requires a completed-pilot reference and digest, verified lineage, and a current provider-paid invoice with a valid bounded period. See [Commercial Truth](COMMERCIAL_TRUTH.md) for the complete existing reconciliation boundaries.

Every active buyer-facing link must land on `thumbgate.ai` first. Raw provider Payment Links are server-side plumbing only. The Aiventyx campaign remains `hold_unverified_cost`; unverified seller fees and downstream obligations block distribution and sending.
