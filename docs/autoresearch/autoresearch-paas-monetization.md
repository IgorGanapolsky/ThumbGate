# Autoresearch PaaS (sfcompute.com) Model & Monetization Strategy

## 1. Key Ideas Extracted from Autoresearch (`autoresearch.sfcompute.com`)
1. **Agent-First Cloud PaaS (Give your LLM a Supercomputer):**
   - A PaaS designed specifically for agent-driven machine learning, providing MCP servers (`https://mcp.autoresearch.sfcompute.com`), interactive H100 nodes, batch jobs, and sweeps.
2. **Transparent, Usage-Based & Discounted Pricing Model:**
   - Granular per-minute pricing for H100-1 ($5.04/hr) and H100-8 ($40.32/hr), with idle windows, sandbox parking discounts, and weekend/Friday discounts (20%-33% off).
3. **Fair Queue Architecture:**
   - Prioritizes small developers and first-time users ahead of whale labs, preventing buy-outs of the entire fleet and capping batch jobs at 85% utilization.
4. **Agent-Native Interfaces:**
   - Everything is CLI/API/MCP-driven (`autor node create`, `autor job submit`), allowing AI agents to provision infrastructure, spin up training sweeps, and top up billing directly.

## 2. Monetization & Revenue Integration for ThumbGate
1. **Autoresearch-Powered Agent Compute Marketplace:**
   - Integrate sfcompute.com MCP patterns into our ThumbGate Pro / Enterprise diagnostic suite so clients can run heavy evaluation runs and fine-tuning sweeps directly through our agent tools.
2. **High-Margin Compute Markup & Retainers:**
   - Offer managed GPU-backed agent sweeps as part of our `$3,000/month` orchestration retainer.
