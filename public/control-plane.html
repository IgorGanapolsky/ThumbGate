What this product is — and what it is not

This is not "sync your chats across machines." CloudCLI already does that for €7/month and does it well. ThumbGate's control plane is the missing reliability layer that sits on top of any session source.

CloudCLI / session browsers

  • Browse and resume sessions across devices ✓
  • Cross-vendor agent support ✓
  • Mobile PWA session view ✓
  • Token cost calculator (community plugin) ✓
  • Stops at "show me what happened"
  • No incident learning across sessions

ThumbGate Control Plane

  • Sees the sessions that are burning budget right now
  • Hot-key approvals before dangerous tool calls
  • Mobile kill switch — stop an agent from your phone
  • Incident history: "your agent tried rm -rf 6 times today"
  • History-aware block rules (ThumbGate) that learn from kills
  • Goes beyond observe — into act on incidents
The wedge: CloudCLI shows you what your agent did. ThumbGate Control Plane shows you the sessions that are burning money or about to force-push main — and lets you stop them remotely.
