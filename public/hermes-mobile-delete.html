Delete your Hermes Mobile data

For Google Play and App Store users of apps published by Igor Ganapolsky (IgorGanapolsky)

This deletion page is for:

  • Hermes Agent Remote & Leash — com.iganapolsky.hermesmobile.paid
  • Hermes AI Agent Leash — com.iganapolsky.hermesmobile

Publisher: Igor Ganapolsky · Play developer name: IgorGanapolsky · Legal entity: Max Smith KDP LLC

Delete data stored on your phone

  1. Open your phone's system settings and select Apps, then Hermes Agent Remote & Leash or Hermes AI Agent Leash.
  2. Select Storage, then Clear storage. You can also uninstall the app.
  3. This removes saved computer profiles, settings, pairing credentials, and other Hermes data stored by the app on that phone.

Request deletion of information you sent us

Hermes Mobile does not create a Hermes, ThumbGate, or Google account inside the app. If you emailed support or otherwise directly gave us personal information, email privacy@thumbgate.ai from the same address with subject Hermes Mobile data deletion and name the app. We will delete support correspondence and other records linked to that address within 30 days, except records we must retain for security, fraud prevention, tax, or other legal obligations.

Anonymous diagnostics

Optional product analytics use a random app-install identifier, not your name, email address, or a Hermes account. Aggregated crash diagnostics are also not attached to a personal account. You can stop new optional analytics at any time in Hermes Settings under Privacy. We do not claim that an email address can identify anonymous records that were never linked to that address.

Data outside the app

  • We cannot reach files on your computer. The Hermes agent on your Mac / Linux / Windows machine keeps its own logs until you delete them there.
  • Play / App Store purchase receipts stay with Google or Apple.
