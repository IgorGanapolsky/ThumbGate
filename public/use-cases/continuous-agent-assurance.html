One loop, three jobs

Assurance lives across the full action lifecycle.

Monitoring alone explains what happened. ThumbGate adds the enforcement point and the verification boundary needed to change what happens next.

01 · OBSERVE

See decision pressure

Review daily evaluated, warned, and denied counts. Empty data stays unknown; it never becomes a safety claim.

→
02 · ENFORCE

Stop the unsafe action

Deterministic checks hold execution authority. Adaptive scores can advise, but they do not silently rewrite policy.

→
03 · VERIFY

Prove the outcome

Attach test results, review state, and task receipts to completion claims. A green-looking process is not outcome proof.

Trust boundaries

Designed to stay boring where it matters.

Content

No raw prompts required

The daily view is constructed from dates and decision counts. Sensitive bodies remain outside the render contract.

Authority

Deterministic checks decide

Learned or predictive components stay advisory until a candidate beats the deterministic baseline and receives approval.

Network

Local-first, offline-capable

The enforcement path does not require a new telemetry vendor or paid model call. No external spend is necessary.

Change control

Feedback proposes; humans govern

A thumbs signal is evidence for review, not permission to widen a rule across every workflow.
