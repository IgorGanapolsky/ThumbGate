FAQ

Why not a SaaS subscription?

Because the open-source CLI does the recurring-revenue work for free. Your prevention rules live in your local SQLite, run on your machine, cost zero monthly. The thing worth paying for is the focused engineering judgment that designs the rule correctly the first time and proves it holds. That's a one-shot Sprint, not a monthly charge.

What if I have multiple repeating failures?

Buy multiple Sprints, one per workflow. Each is fixed-scope. We've found that bundling produces worse outcomes because rules drift when designed in batch.

Can you sign a SOW / MSA / NDA?

Yes — standard MSA + SOW for the engagement, NDA on request. Government and regulated-industry teams: see the federal pilot lane.
