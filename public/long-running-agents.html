Why deterministic matters more than aligned

Alignment training and content filters answer "what is the model statistically likely to say?" That is a probabilistic property. It produces a residual error rate that, for regulated workloads, translates directly into incident exposure. Pre-Action Checks answer a different question: "is the specific tool call about to execute on the allowed list?" That answer is binary. The check fires or it does not. There is no temperature setting, no sampling, no model decision involved. That is the deterministic assurance bar boards and risk committees can sign.

ThumbGate does not replace alignment. It runs after the model has decided what to do and before the runtime executes it. The model can still be wrong; the gate catches the consequence.
