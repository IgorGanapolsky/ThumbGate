Privacy Policy & Data Protection Disclosure

Last Updated: August 12, 2026 · Version 1.3.0
Zero Workspace Telemetry Guarantee. The local thumbgate CLI and open-source engine operate on your host disk (~/.thumbgate/, .claude/). Zero workspace source code, repository contents, or project file data is fetched, transmitted, or publicly rendered by ThumbGate.

1. What Stays Local

  • Your source code, local repositories, and workspace files.
  • Local prevention rules, context packs, and lesson store.
  • Local MCP execution traces and shell command history.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Account data is retained while the account remains active. You may request deletion or export by emailing privacy@thumbgate.ai. Cloud runner logs are purged automatically after 30 days. Local data is controlled by you; deleting local directories removes it permanently.

5. Enterprise Data Processing Addendum

Enterprise customers may request a DPA covering GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA. ThumbGate acts as a processor, processing data only on your documented instructions. Security measures include TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, RBAC, MFA, and zero-trust administrative access.

6. Security Incident Notification

We will notify your designated security contact without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after confirming a security incident affecting your personal or operational metadata.

7. International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We implement Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission for cross-border transfers.

8. Data Portability

Upon termination, enterprise customers may request a machine-readable export of account metadata, cloud logs within the retention window, and configured rule schemas. ThumbGate will provide the export within 30 days and delete remaining cloud-hosted data within 90 days, except where longer retention is required by law.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries, DPA requests, or data subject access requests: privacy@thumbgate.ai · security@thumbgate.ai
