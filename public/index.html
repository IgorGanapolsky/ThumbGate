Self-improving product loop

Pre-action checks—and the systems that refine them.

No governance novel. Corrections become reviewable local lessons; relevant lessons are re-ranked; repeated negative patterns can become explicit gates; and the next action is checked before execution.

1

Capture feedback

Record explicit 👍 or 👎 feedback with the action and outcome context that made it useful or wrong.

2

Remember locally

Store a reviewable lesson that survives sessions and model changes without sending the control history into model weights.

3

Rank and refine

Re-rank relevant lessons for the action. Repeated negative patterns can promote from warnings to blocking gates; stale gates expire.

4

Gate the next action

The action is allowed, warned, or denied before the tool proceeds.
