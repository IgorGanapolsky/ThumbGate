How the pilot runs

Your agent proposes actions. Your team decides which ones it may repeat. Every decision is written down, so at the end you have a record, not an impression.

  1. Start with one repo.Run npx thumbgate init in the repository where an agent mistake would hurt most. Nothing else changes.
  2. Your team decides.When the agent does something wrong, give it a thumbs-down. That becomes a prevention rule, and ThumbGate warns or blocks the next repeat before it runs.
  3. Evidence for every decision.Each allow, warn and block is logged on your machine. npx thumbgate inventory turns the log into a report: decisions per tool, the gates that blocked most, activity per day and the false-block rate.
  4. Decide at day 30.Review the report with us. Add more repos, keep it on one, or stop. The rules and the log stay with you.
ThumbGate is a pre-action check for AI coding agents: it blocks a known-bad action before it runs.
