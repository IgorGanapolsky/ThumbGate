Unattended mutations stay off until an admin enables them. An inbound mention does not grant a tool, an automatic review cannot approve or merge, a permission does not transfer across identities, and a raw capture is refused once a durable note exists. Compare-not-clone: this does not install Codex, a ChatGPT plugin, Bedrock, or Daybreak, and the gate does not call a model.
