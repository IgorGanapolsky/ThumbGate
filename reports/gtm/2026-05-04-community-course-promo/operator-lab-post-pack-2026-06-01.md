## Post 2 — “What is a Pre-Action Gate?”

A Pre-Action Gate is a check that runs before the risky action.

Instead of hoping the agent “remembers”, we enforce:

- what it’s allowed to do
- what it must validate first
- what it must log for proof

Post one repeated failure you want blocked and the proof you want after.
