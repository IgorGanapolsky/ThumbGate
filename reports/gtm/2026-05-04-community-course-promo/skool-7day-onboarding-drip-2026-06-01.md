## Day 1 — Common failures (pick one)

Which one is blocking you right now?

- unsafe tool calls
- missing approval boundaries
- context drift / stale instructions

## Day 2 — “Smallest enforceable gate”

If you can only enforce one thing this week, enforce the pre-action check that prevents the costly failure.

Post your candidate gate in one sentence.

## Day 3 — Proof question

What would make you trust the fix?

- tests passing
- zero unsafe tool calls in a week
- reproducible before/after
- auditable logs
