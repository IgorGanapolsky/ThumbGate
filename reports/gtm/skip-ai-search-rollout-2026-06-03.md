## Why Skip Helps

Skip is not a replacement for ThumbGate-owned SEO. Its value is a low-cost crawlable distribution surface:

- Public business listing: another indexed entity page that says ThumbGate = pre-action checks for AI agents.
- Product/service showcase: a specific page for the workflow-hardening sprint instead of only a generic company profile.
- Blog/social scheduling: a weekly cadence that repeats the same brand-to-problem association across Google, AI search, and social surfaces.
- Dashboard measurement: a quick read on impressions and clicks from a channel that is cheaper than MarkTechPost.

Official Skip docs say public listings are intended to boost SEO and visibility, and product/service showcases can appear across Skip discovery surfaces after manual review.
