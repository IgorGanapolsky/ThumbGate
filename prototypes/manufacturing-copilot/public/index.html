ThumbGate: The Infrastructure Firewall for AI Copilots

Securing Plant 7 Operations against RAG Poisoning, Safety Violations, and Hallucinations

⚠️ The Operational Risk

  • RAG Context Poisoning:

    Adversaries or legacy errors inside maintenance manuals instruct LLMs to override lockout/tagout (LOTO).

  • Direct Injections:

    Operators attempting to bypass safety rules or extract system instructions.

  • Data Leaks (PII):

    Employee IDs or emails leaking into external LLM prompts and API logs.

  • Hallucinations:

    LLM generating instructions based on low-confidence retrieval matches when docs are missing.

🛡️ Multi-Layered Defense

1
Pre-Model Protection

PII redaction & direct injection blocks. Poisoned RAG chunks are quarantined before model read.

2
In-Flight Gating

Retrieval confidence threshold. If match score is below limit, request is refused & escalated.

3
Post-Model Enforcement

Unsafe output scan (blocks bypass instructions) + safety citation gate (enforces SP-xxx procedure codes).

📈 Business Value & ROI

  • Accident Prevention:

    Guarantees zero-bypass instructions reach the plant floor, avoiding injury and costly downtime.

  • 100% Traceability & Audit:

    LangSmith middleware records latency, inputs, outputs, and gate decisions for compliance reporting.

  • Local-First Sovereignty:

    Operates entirely on-premises or within private hybrid cloud. Data stays local; no third-party RAG risks.

  • Self-Improving Guardrails:

    Pre-action gates turn operator thumbs-down/mistakes into permanent local prevention rules autonomously.
