# Public OSHA Maintenance And Machine Safety Source Index

These chunks replace synthetic plant maintenance instructions with public OSHA
machine-safety guidance. Page references use PDF page numbers from local OSHA
source files under `data/sources/`.

## OSHA-3170: Hazardous Maintenance Activities
<!-- source_title: OSHA 3170 Safeguarding Equipment and Protecting Employees from Amputations -->
<!-- source_url: https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3170.pdf -->
<!-- source_pdf: data/sources/OSHA3170-amputation-machine-guarding.pdf -->
<!-- source_page: 11 -->

Machine setup, inspection, clearing jams, adjustment, cleaning, lubrication,
and scheduled or unscheduled maintenance can present amputation hazards. Safe
maintenance guidance should tell supervisors to use approved safeguarding and
energy-control procedures rather than improvising around guards or interlocks.

## OSHA-3170: Power Press Servicing And LOTO
<!-- source_title: OSHA 3170 Safeguarding Equipment and Protecting Employees from Amputations -->
<!-- source_url: https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3170.pdf -->
<!-- source_pdf: data/sources/OSHA3170-amputation-machine-guarding.pdf -->
<!-- source_page: 24 -->

OSHA identifies failure to disable, isolate, and lockout/tagout press energy
sources before servicing or maintenance as a power-press hazard. Supervisor
answers about press service should cite the need for energy isolation and
authorized maintenance involvement, not provide press-start, override, or
interlock-bypass commands.

## OSHA-3170: Conveyors Need Guarding And Energy Control
<!-- source_title: OSHA 3170 Safeguarding Equipment and Protecting Employees from Amputations -->
<!-- source_url: https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3170.pdf -->
<!-- source_pdf: data/sources/OSHA3170-amputation-machine-guarding.pdf -->
<!-- source_page: 30 -->

Conveyors present amputation hazards associated with mechanical motion. OSHA
states employers need to evaluate each conveyor and determine the required
primary safeguarding methods and energy-control practices. Floor supervisors
can request status and escalate maintenance, but physical conveyor stop/start
commands must be gated as tool calls.

## OSHA-3170: Milling Machine Safeguards
<!-- source_title: OSHA 3170 Safeguarding Equipment and Protecting Employees from Amputations -->
<!-- source_url: https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3170.pdf -->
<!-- source_pdf: data/sources/OSHA3170-amputation-machine-guarding.pdf -->
<!-- source_page: 46 -->

OSHA machine-tool guidance describes amputation risks around milling machines
and the need to prevent employee contact with rotating cutters and hazardous
motion. Supervisors can ask what safeguards are for, whether a machine should
be escalated, or whether maintenance should inspect it; bypass and restart
actions remain blocked.

## OSHA-3170: Applicable Standards For Machine Safety
<!-- source_title: OSHA 3170 Safeguarding Equipment and Protecting Employees from Amputations -->
<!-- source_url: https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3170.pdf -->
<!-- source_pdf: data/sources/OSHA3170-amputation-machine-guarding.pdf -->
<!-- source_page: 54 -->

OSHA lists standards relevant to machine safety, including control of hazardous
energy, machine guarding requirements, and mechanical power-transmission
apparatus. A cited answer should point supervisors to these governing source
families and avoid inventing plant-specific maintenance steps that are not in
the source material.
