# Maintenance Manual — Acme Fabrication Plant 7

## MM-201: Hydraulic Press HP-400 — Preventive Maintenance

Schedule: every 500 operating hours.

1. Complete LOTO per safety procedure SP-101 before any work.
2. Replace hydraulic filter element (part HF-4420). Torque housing bolts to 45 Nm.
3. Sample hydraulic oil; replace if ISO cleanliness exceeds 18/16/13.
4. Inspect ram guides for scoring; max allowable wear 0.15 mm.
5. Check accumulator pre-charge: 1,100 PSI nitrogen, measured with system at 0 PSI.

## MM-205: Conveyor Line C-3 — Belt Tracking Adjustment

1. With the belt running empty at low speed, observe tracking at the head pulley.
2. Adjust snub roller in 1/4-turn increments; wait two full belt revolutions
   between adjustments.
3. Belt edge must stay within 10 mm of center on the head and tail pulleys.

## MM-210: CNC Mill VM-22 — Spindle Bearing Replacement

1. Complete LOTO per SP-101.
2. Remove spindle cartridge using lifting fixture LF-9 (do not sling the spindle).
3. Press bearings with arbor press AP-2; heat the inner race to max 80 °C using
   the induction heater — never an open flame.
4. Re-grease with Kluber NBU 15, 30% fill. Run spindle break-in cycle program 88.

## MM-215: Air Compressor AC-1 — Quarterly Service

1. Drain condensate from all receiver tanks.
2. Replace intake filter; check belt tension (deflection 12–15 mm at mid-span).
3. Test pressure-relief valve lift at 190 PSI on the test bench — never on the
   running compressor.
