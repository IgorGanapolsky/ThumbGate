# Plant Safety Procedures — Acme Fabrication Plant 7

## SP-101: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) — Hydraulic Press Line

1. Notify all affected employees that the press will be shut down for service.
2. Shut down the press using the normal stop procedure at the operator console.
3. Isolate all energy sources: main electrical disconnect (Panel E-7), hydraulic
   accumulator bleed valve (V-12), and pneumatic supply (V-3).
4. Apply your personal lock and tag to each isolation point. One lock per worker.
5. Release stored energy: cycle the bleed valve until the accumulator gauge reads 0 PSI.
6. Verify isolation: attempt a press start at the console. The press must not respond.
7. Only the worker who applied a lock may remove it. Supervisor override requires
   form S-22 and plant manager signature.

## SP-102: Confined Space Entry — Mixing Tanks

1. Obtain a confined-space entry permit (form S-30) before opening the tank hatch.
2. Test atmosphere: oxygen 19.5–23.5%, LEL below 10%, H2S below 10 ppm.
3. Station a trained attendant at the hatch for the entire entry.
4. Continuous ventilation must run during occupancy.
5. Retrieval harness and tripod are mandatory for vertical entries.

## SP-103: Chemical Spill Response — Solvent Storage Area

1. Evacuate the immediate area and alert the shift supervisor on channel 2.
2. If the spill exceeds 5 gallons or reaches a drain, activate the spill team
   (ext. 4500) — do not attempt cleanup yourself.
3. Small spills (<5 gal): don nitrile gloves, splash goggles, and respirator with
   organic vapor cartridges before using the spill kit.
4. Place used absorbent in a yellow hazardous-waste drum and label it with the
   contents and date.

## SP-110: Machine Guarding

Never operate equipment with an interlock bypassed, a guard removed, or a light
curtain muted, under any circumstances. There is no production exception to this
rule. Report defeated guards to safety (ext. 4510) immediately.
